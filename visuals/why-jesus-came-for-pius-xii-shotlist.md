60 scenes · runtime ≈ 20:14 · timestamps at 145 wpm (re-time to the final voiceover)

**Asset key:** ARCH = real archival photo/film/print · STOCK = real location/stock footage · AI = AI atmosphere (objects, rooms; never real people's faces) · RECON = stylised painterly reconstruction (tag "RECONSTRUCTION" small, top-right) · GFX = motion graphic

**Type system:** high-contrast serif (e.g. Playfair Display) for dates, numbers and quotes · bold sans-serif (e.g. Inter) for names and labels · white text only · crimson (#B3202A) as the single accent · lower-third = round B&W portrait, then name, then one-line descriptor, bottom-left, over a soft vignette.

**Source cards:** paper-texture clipping with the publication name typeset in plain serif (no copied mastheads) + date + paraphrased point. Never put invented quotes in quotation marks.

## Thumbnail concepts

**A: "Came for him" (recommended)**
- Image: an archival B&W Pius XII portrait (real) on the left, eyes up. On the right, a soft painterly backlit figure (RECON style, no detailed face). The two are split by warm golden light.
- Text: **NOT YET** (white, heavy condensed, with YET in crimson). Avoid "HE SAID NO": the record never says Christ refused him.
- Badge: small "VATICAN CONFIRMED" tag, top-left.

**B: The bed**
- Image: an empty papal bed in dark gold light, with a beam of light falling on the pillow. A small inset of the Pius portrait.
- Text: **STILL ALIVE** (with ALIVE in crimson).

**C: The overrule**
- Image: the Pius portrait with a crimson "NO COMMENT" stamp crossed out over a Vatican flag.
- Text: **HE OVERRULED THEM** (with THEM in crimson).

Thumbnail rules: no more than three words of text, one crimson word, a real face, and nothing that implies a miracle cure.
