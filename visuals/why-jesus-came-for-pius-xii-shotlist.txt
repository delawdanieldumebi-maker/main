VISUAL PLAN: WHY JESUS CAME FOR POPE PIUS XII... AND DIDN'T TAKE HIM

60 scenes. Runtime about 20:14. Timestamps assume 145 words per minute, so re-time them to the final voiceover.

ASSET KEY
ARCH: real archival photos, film or prints.
STOCK: real location or stock footage.
AI: AI-generated atmosphere (objects and rooms only, never the faces of real people).
RECON: stylised painterly reconstruction, with a small RECONSTRUCTION tag in the top-right corner.
GFX: motion graphics.

TYPE AND COLOUR
Serif (for example Playfair Display) for dates, numbers and quotes. Bold sans-serif (for example Inter) for names and labels. White text only. One crimson accent (#B3202A).
Lower-thirds: a round black-and-white portrait, then the name, then a one-line descriptor, bottom-left over a soft vignette.
Source cards: paper-texture clippings with the publication name typeset in plain serif (no copied mastheads), the date, and a paraphrased point. Never put invented quotes in quotation marks.

THUMBNAIL CONCEPTS

A: "Came for him" (recommended)
- Image: an archival B&W Pius XII portrait (real) on the left, eyes up. On the right, a soft painterly backlit figure (RECON style, no detailed face). The two are split by warm golden light.
- Text: NOT YET (white, heavy condensed, with YET in crimson). Avoid "HE SAID NO": the record never says Christ refused him.
- Badge: small "VATICAN CONFIRMED" tag, top-left.

B: The bed
- Image: an empty papal bed in dark gold light, with a beam of light falling on the pillow. A small inset of the Pius portrait.
- Text: STILL ALIVE (with ALIVE in crimson).

C: The overrule
- Image: the Pius portrait with a crimson "NO COMMENT" stamp crossed out over a Vatican flag.
- Text: HE OVERRULED THEM (with THEM in crimson).

Thumbnail rules: no more than three words of text, one crimson word, a real face, and nothing that implies a miracle cure.
