# Scene: The Midnight Platform

**Duration:** 30 seconds
**Aesthetic:** 1980s Cold War spy thriller — dark, foggy, tense. Entire film in black and white. B&W film grade applied to all still scene references before video generation.

Footsteps echo — a young courier in her mid-20s named Sonia walks up and stops a few feet from him, facing the empty tracks rather than him. Neither looks at the other.

Mr. Volkov exhales smoke and asks, without turning, whether the shipment left on schedule. She slides a folded photograph along the top of a nearby luggage cart between them, then turns and walks briskly back the way she came, disappearing into the fog without a backward glance.

Mr. Volkov palms the photograph, studies it for a beat under the lamp's flicker, then slips it into his coat's breast pocket. He drops his cigarette, crushes it underfoot, and walks off in the opposite direction down the platform — leaving a single unclaimed suitcase behind at his feet.
