# Video Generation Prompts: "The Midnight Platform"

Six clips, ~5 seconds each, 30 seconds total. Each prompt is self-contained for image-to-video generation — feed each the matching graded still from the production brief as the seed frame, and keep character/location reference assets locked across all six so faces, wardrobe, fog density, and lamp flicker stay consistent.

---

## Clip 1 — Establishing Shot (0:00–0:05)

**Prompt:**
Cinematic black-and-white nighttime scene, abandoned train station platform. Wide static shot. Thick fog drifts low across empty railroad tracks, illuminated by a single overhead lamp that flickers unevenly, casting hard pools of light against deep shadow. Mr. Volkov, a weary man in his late 40s with a weathered face and close-cropped greying hair, stands alone at the platform's edge, wearing a dark wool overcoat with the collar turned up against the cold. He holds a lit cigarette between his fingers, slowly raising it to take a slow drag, exhaling visible smoke into the cold air. His posture is tense, watchful, eyes scanning the dark beyond the platform. Camera holds still, barely perceptible handheld micro-movement for tension. High contrast lighting, deep crushed blacks, soft highlight bloom around the lamp, heavy 35mm film grain, subtle vignette. Slow, deliberate pacing. Ambient sound: distant wind, faint metallic creak of the station structure, soft crackle of the cigarette.

**Duration:** 5s | **Camera:** Static wide | **Characters:** Mr. Volkov | **Audio:** Ambient wind, distant creak, no dialogue

---

## Clip 2 — Sonia Arrives (0:05–0:10)

**Prompt:**
Cinematic black-and-white nighttime scene, same abandoned train station platform, fog and flickering overhead lamp. Medium-wide shot. Footsteps echo softly against concrete as Sonia, a composed young woman in her mid-20s with dark hair pulled back and a long dark trench coat, enters frame from the fog and walks toward Mr. Volkov, who remains standing at the platform's edge with his cigarette. She stops a few feet from him. Neither character turns to face the other — both look outward toward the empty tracks, bodies angled away from each other, conveying wariness and practiced tradecraft discipline. Her expression is tense but controlled, jaw tight, eyes downcast. Camera performs a slow, subtle push-in on the two-shot. High contrast black-and-white grade, deep shadows, fog diffusing the light into soft halos, heavy film grain, moderate vignette. Ambient sound: footsteps on concrete, echo fading, distant wind, no dialogue yet.

**Duration:** 5s | **Camera:** Slow push-in, medium-wide two-shot | **Characters:** Mr. Volkov, Sonia | **Audio:** Footsteps, echo, wind, no dialogue

---

## Clip 3 — The Question (0:10–0:15)

**Prompt:**
Cinematic black-and-white nighttime scene, train station platform, fog and flickering lamp overhead. Medium two-shot, both characters still facing the tracks, not each other. Mr. Volkov exhales a slow stream of cigarette smoke, then speaks quietly without turning his head toward Sonia: "Did the shipment leave on schedule?" His voice is low, flat, controlled. Sonia's expression shifts almost imperceptibly — a beat of hesitation, a slight tightening around her eyes, before she remains silent, gathering herself to answer. Minimal camera movement, near-static medium shot holding the tension between them. High contrast black-and-white grade, hard lamplight flicker catching the smoke as it drifts, deep shadow pooling around their feet, heavy film grain, soft vignette. Ambient sound: faint wind, distant lamp buzz/flicker sound, then Volkov's whispered line, followed by a tense pause.

**Duration:** 5s | **Camera:** Static medium two-shot | **Characters:** Mr. Volkov, Sonia | **Audio/Dialogue:** Mr. Volkov: "Did the shipment leave on schedule?"

---

## Clip 4 — The Warning and the Drop (0:15–0:20)

**Prompt:**
Cinematic black-and-white nighttime scene, train station platform, fog and flickering overhead lamp. Medium shot transitioning to wide as Sonia exits. Sonia speaks quietly, still not making eye contact, her voice low and urgent: "It never left. They rerouted it through the border checkpoint — dawn. Warn the others before then." As she finishes speaking, she reaches to a nearby luggage cart positioned between her and Mr. Volkov, and slides a folded photograph along its top surface toward him with a quick, practiced motion. Immediately after, she turns sharply and walks briskly back the way she came, her trench coat moving with her stride, disappearing into the drifting fog without a backward glance. Camera holds on Mr. Volkov briefly, then follows her retreating silhouette as it dissolves into the mist. High contrast black-and-white grade, fog swallowing her silhouette progressively, hard lamp flicker, heavy grain, vignette darkening toward the edges as she recedes. Ambient sound: her whispered dialogue, brisk footsteps receding, fading into silence and wind.

**Duration:** 5s | **Camera:** Medium holding, then following wide as she exits | **Characters:** Sonia (primary), Mr. Volkov | **Audio/Dialogue:** Sonia: "It never left. They rerouted it through the border checkpoint — dawn. Warn the others before then."

## Clip 5 — The Photograph (0:20–0:25)

**Prompt:**
Cinematic black-and-white nighttime scene, train station platform, fog and flickering overhead lamp, now alone. Close-up/insert shot on Mr. Volkov's hands and face. He reaches down and palms the folded photograph left on the luggage cart, unfolding it slightly and studying it for a beat under the unstable flicker of the lamp above — his expression hardening, eyes narrowing with recognition or concern. He then folds it closed and slips it into his coat's inner breast pocket with a practiced, discreet motion, patting the pocket once. Camera is a tight, slow, static close-up, occasionally catching the lamp's flicker reflected in his eyes. High contrast black-and-white grade, strong directional light from above catching the photograph's surface, deep shadow elsewhere, heavy film grain, tight vignette framing his face and hands. Ambient sound: paper rustling faintly, distant wind, lamp buzz, no dialogue.

**Duration:** 5s | **Camera:** Static close-up/insert | **Characters:** Mr. Volkov | **Audio:** Paper rustle, ambient wind/lamp buzz, no dialogue

---

## Clip 6 — The Exit (0:25–0:30)

**Prompt:**
Cinematic black-and-white nighttime scene, train station platform, fog and flickering overhead lamp. Wide static final shot. Mr. Volkov drops his spent cigarette to the concrete platform and crushes it underfoot with a deliberate, weary motion. He then turns and walks off down the platform in the opposite direction from where Sonia exited, his silhouette gradually swallowed by drifting fog, footsteps echoing and fading. He leaves behind a single unclaimed suitcase sitting alone at the spot where he stood, now lit starkly by the flickering lamp above with no one beside it. Camera holds static on the wide platform as he recedes, ending on the abandoned suitcase under the flickering light as the final image. High contrast black-and-white grade, deep crushed blacks, fog thickening at the frame's edges, heavy film grain, strong vignette closing in around the isolated suitcase. Ambient sound: footsteps receding into silence, distant wind, faint lamp flicker/buzz, no dialogue.

**Duration:** 5s | **Camera:** Static wide, ending on suitcase insert/hold | **Characters:** Mr. Volkov | **Audio:** Footsteps receding, wind, lamp buzz, no dialogue

## Continuity Reminders

- Feed each clip's matching graded still (from the production brief) as the seed/reference image — do not regenerate characters from text description alone per clip.
- Keep the same locked character reference (face, coat, hair) and location plate across all six prompts; only the action/camera/dialogue lines change.
- Keep grain size, contrast level, and vignette strength identical across clips — apply the same LUT/preset before generating motion, not after.
- If your video tool supports negative prompts, exclude: color, oversaturation, modern signage, clean/sharp digital look, motion blur artifacts, warped hands/faces, extra limbs, text overlays.
