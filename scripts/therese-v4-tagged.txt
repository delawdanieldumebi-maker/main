Now, the young man who died in that hospital.

On the 15th of October, 1895, a letter arrived at the Carmel from a twenty-one-year-old seminarian named Maurice Bellière. He hoped to become a missionary, and he was asking for one thing: a nun who would pray for him.

The prioress was Thérèse's own sister Pauline. She gave the job to Thérèse.

Thérèse had once had two brothers. Both died as babies. Now, suddenly, she had one again. She prayed for Maurice, she wrote to him, and she took him seriously.

A year later she was given a second brother, a young priest named Adolphe Roulland, who was being sent to China. Instead, something started that nobody at Lisieux had planned. And it put her promise on trial.
