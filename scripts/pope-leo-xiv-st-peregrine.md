## 11. Why him

Which brings us back to the question we started with.

Why Peregrine? When the Pope is sick, why does the Church turn to a saint who was once the Pope's enemy?

There are three reasons. And each one says something about us.

The first is forgiveness. Peregrine is only a saint because someone forgave him first. If Philip Benizi had hit back, cursed him or reported him, there would be no St. Peregrine. There would just be one more angry young man in a rebel city. But Philip's forgiveness outlived Philip. The friar died in 1285, and the man he forgave went on healing and comforting people for sixty years. Seven hundred years later, that same forgiveness is praying for a pope. We never know how far one act of mercy will travel. Philip never saw Peregrine become a saint. All he had to do was forgive.
